RESEARCH

Research Areas

Dr. Rosalin Sahoo's research encompasses advanced composite structures, structural mechanics, computational mechanics and uncertainty quantification, with emphasis on analytical and numerical approaches for structural response prediction.

RESEARCH THEMES

Areas of Research

The research activities cover material development, structural modelling, computational analysis and performance assessment of advanced engineering structures.

01

Laminated & Sandwich Composites

Research on laminated composite materials, sandwich constructions and their structural behaviour under different loading and environmental conditions.

→
02

Plate & Shell Structures

Analytical and numerical investigation of plate and shell structures using advanced structural theories for accurate response prediction.

→
03

Smart & Advanced Composites

Investigation of functionally graded materials, CNT-reinforced composites, graphene-based composites and other advanced structural materials.

→
04

Structural Analysis

Studies involving bending, buckling, free vibration, dynamic response and stability of advanced structural systems.

→
05

Finite Element Methods

Development and application of finite element and analytical approaches for efficient modelling and analysis of complex structural systems.

→
06

Uncertainty Quantification

Investigation of the influence of material, geometric and other parametric uncertainties on structural response and performance.

→
