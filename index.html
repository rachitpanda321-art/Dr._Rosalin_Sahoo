ABOUT

Dr. Rosalin Sahoo

Associate Professor

Department of Civil Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi

Dr. Rosalin Sahoo is an Associate Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi. Her academic and research work focuses on advanced composite structures, structural mechanics and computational methods.

She completed her Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur in 2015. Her research encompasses laminated and sandwich composites, plate and shell structures, smart composites, finite element methods and uncertainty quantification.
