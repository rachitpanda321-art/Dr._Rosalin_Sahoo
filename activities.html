International Conference 2–4 December 2025

8th International Conference on Recent Advances in Composite Materials (ICRACM-2025)

ABLT Complex, IIT (BHU), Varanasi

The Department of Civil Engineering, IIT (BHU), successfully hosted ICRACM-2025, a three-day international conference focused on recent developments in composite materials and their emerging applications.

Conference Highlights
  • Around 200 participants attended the event.
  • Around 170 oral presentations were delivered across 24 parallel technical tracks.
  • 20 keynote lectures were delivered by distinguished national and international experts.
  • The conference covered manufacturing, applications, uncertainty modelling, environmental considerations and future trends in composite materials.

International Workshop 23–24 December 2024

International Workshop on “Modelling and Structural Analysis of Advanced Materials”

IIT (BHU), Varanasi

The workshop was jointly organised by the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT (BHU), in collaboration with the University of Alabama, USA, under the SPARC initiative.

Workshop Highlights
  • Distinguished lectures by invited experts.
  • Interactive technical discussions.
  • Academic and research networking among participants from across the country.
  • Focus on modelling and structural analysis of advanced materials.
